Innov-8 is announcing **i8 Precision** — a new feature coming soon to the
i8panel platform that changes how businesses schedule and manage field service
visits.

This concept has been a strategic priority for CEO and Founder Mike Wolfenden
for some time. "The real challenge wasn't the idea itself, but viable
delivery," he explains. "It required the right partnership ecosystem and
overcoming significant technical and operational barriers. Through our
collaboration with the industry's leading field agencies, we've finally
achieved what once seemed impossible."

## What is i8 Precision?

i8 Precision enables clients to schedule their own site visits within the
i8panel platform, choosing from defined 2-hour windows at dates and times that
work best for them. Whether it's a confirmed appointment or a targeted contact
attempt, every case type is covered.

The capability is built for:

- **Debt Collection Agencies (DCAs) and Debt Practitioners (DPs)** — optimise debt recovery strategies
- **Utility companies** — control meter installation, inspection, and dispute resolution visits
- **Litigators** — execute time-sensitive legal processes
- **Banks and lenders** — manage property inspections and asset verification
- **Councils** — coordinate welfare checks and enforcement visits

## Why this matters

The flexibility of i8 Precision means you're no longer constrained by standard
availability windows. Want to schedule a site visit for the exact date and time
you believe gives you the best chance to engage a customer? Now you can.

Do your customers prefer to give you access to their property on their own
terms? i8 Precision accommodates that. Need a same-day or next-day site visit?
It's possible.

From high balance debt and broken arrangements, to known vulnerability and
self-disconnected prepayment meter investigations, through to urgent or
emergency visits — i8 Precision covers every case type.

This isn't a fit for every site visit, and it comes with a premium fee. But for
organisations that need maximum control and flexibility in field service
delivery, it puts you closer to your field partners and in full command of the
service experience.

The premium investment delivers:

- **You're in control** — pick the day and 2-hour window that works for your property and visit type
- **Built for every case type** — from confirmed appointments to targeted contact attempts, all workflows are covered
- **Flexibility** — book a confirmed appointment, or target the best time to make contact. Either way, you decide the timing
