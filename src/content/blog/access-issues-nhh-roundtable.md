On Friday 25 September 2026, we're hosting a roundtable session with industry
water wholesalers to tackle one of the sector's most pressing topics: access
issues in the non-household water market.

It's not often you can bring together almost an entire industry to a single
table. The fact that we've managed to do so speaks volumes about how
significant this issue is, and how urgent the need for solutions has become.

## Why this matters now

With AMP8 pledging to install massive volumes of smart meters over the next few
years — and AMP9 beyond that — access to non-household sites has become
critical. The numbers are sobering: **over 50% of businesses are currently
either non-engaging or refusing to have smart meters installed.**

Beyond smart meter rollout, site access is essential for:

- Resolving long unread meters
- Leakage detection and resolution
- Non-payment cases (retailer services)
- General wholesaler and retailer access requests
- Delegation of authority and Rights of Entry warrant processes

The same challenges will mirror the household sector, where take-up and
installation rates are equally critical to achieving ambitious AMP targets.
